# Start Here — Receipt Shoebox

You have a zip file. Here is how to get it in front of your AI. Pick your
device.

## iPhone or iPad

1. Open the **Files** app and find the zip you downloaded.
2. **Tap the zip** — it unzips into a folder with the same name.
3. Open that folder and find **`installer.md`**.
4. Tap and hold `installer.md` → **Share** → choose your **Claude** or
   **ChatGPT** app. (If sharing into the app isn't offered, open the file, copy
   all its text, and paste it into a new chat.)

## Windows

1. **Right-click** the zip → **Extract All** → **Extract**.
2. Open the folder that appears.
3. Find **`installer.md`** and **drag it** into your AI app or the AI website in
   your browser. (Or open it in Notepad, select all, copy, and paste into a new
   chat.)

## Mac

1. **Double-click** the zip — it unzips into a folder next to it.
2. Open the folder and find **`installer.md`**.
3. **Drag `installer.md`** into your AI app or the AI website in your browser.
   (Or open it, copy all the text, and paste it into a new chat.)

---

Then send that file to your AI and say: **install receipt shoebox.**
