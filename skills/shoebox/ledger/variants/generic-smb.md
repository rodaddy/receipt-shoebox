# Category Variant -- Generic Small Business

**For:** a business that fits none of the other three variants. This is the
neutral office list. If the business buys materials for customer jobs, sells
product, or bills time, use one of the specific variants instead -- they are
worth real money at tax time and this one is not.

To use it, copy everything under "Starter categories" below into
`../categories.md`, replacing whatever list is there.

## Starter categories

- **Direct Job / Client Costs** -- anything bought for one specific customer,
  job, or project rather than for the business generally. Even in a generic
  business this is the line worth separating, because it is what tells you
  whether a piece of work made money.
- **Subcontractors & Contract Labor**
- **Office Supplies**
- **Software & Subscriptions**
- **Equipment & Hardware** -- computers, monitors, phones, tools.
- **Equipment Purchase (capital -- ask your accountant about depreciating)**
- **Equipment Repair & Maintenance** -- repairs and service, kept out of
  Vehicle & Fuel.
- **Vehicle & Fuel** -- fuel, tolls, parking. Fuel only.
- **Travel** -- flights, hotels, rideshare, trains.
- **Client Meals**
- **Team Meals & Office Food**
- **Marketing & Advertising**
- **Professional Services** -- legal, accounting, consulting.
- **Utilities & Phone**
- **Rent & Facilities**
- **Shipping & Postage**
- **Insurance**
- **Education & Training**
- **Permits, Licenses & Filing Fees**
- **Bank & Merchant Fees**
- **Other / Uncategorized**

## Judgment notes

- The job field still applies: ask which customer, project, or job a purchase
  was for whenever one is open.
- A repair shop is **Equipment Repair & Maintenance**, never Vehicle & Fuel.
- Any single item over $2,500 gets a capital-vs-expense note written into the
  row (see `../../SKILL.md`).
