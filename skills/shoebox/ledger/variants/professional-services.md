# Category Variant -- Professional Services

**For:** consultants, agencies, bookkeepers, law and insurance offices,
architects, anyone who bills time and expertise rather than materials.

To use it, copy everything under "Starter categories" below into
`../categories.md`, replacing whatever list is there.

## Starter categories

**Client-related spend (the money questions).**

- **Client Direct Costs (billable)** -- anything bought specifically for one
  client and expected to be rebilled or recovered: filing fees, courier,
  research subscriptions bought for a matter, printing for a client deliverable,
  travel charged through. This is the closest thing this business has to cost of
  goods sold, and it is the number that tells you whether an engagement made
  money.
- **Subcontractors & Contract Labor** -- a freelancer, of-counsel, designer, or
  specialist paid to do part of a client's work.
- **Client Meals & Entertainment** -- meals with a client or referral partner.
  Kept separate from team meals because they are treated differently.
- **Team Meals & Office Food** -- coffee, lunches, office supplies people eat.

**Everything else.**

- **Software & Subscriptions** -- apps, hosting, research databases, AI tools.
- **Office Supplies**
- **Equipment & Hardware** -- computers, monitors, phones.
- **Equipment Purchase (capital -- ask your accountant about depreciating)** --
  a large hardware or furniture purchase bought outright.
- **Marketing & Advertising**
- **Professional Services** -- your own legal, accounting, consulting.
- **Utilities & Phone**
- **Rent & Facilities** -- office, coworking, storage.
- **Insurance** -- professional liability, general liability.
- **Education & Training** -- courses, CLE, certifications, conferences.
- **Licenses, Dues & Filing Fees** -- bar dues, professional memberships,
  state filings.
- **Travel** -- flights, hotels, rideshare, trains.
- **Vehicle & Fuel** -- business mileage-related fuel, tolls, parking.
- **Bank & Merchant Fees**
- **Other / Uncategorized**

## Judgment notes specific to this business

- The key question is not "which job" but "which client or matter" -- the job
  field still applies and should be answered with the client or matter name.
- A subscription bought for one client is **Client Direct Costs (billable)**;
  the same subscription bought for the whole firm is **Software &
  Subscriptions**. Ask which it was when it is not obvious.
- Any single item over $2,500 gets a capital-vs-expense note written into the
  row (see `../../SKILL.md`).
