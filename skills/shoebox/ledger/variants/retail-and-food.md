# Category Variant -- Retail and Food Service

**For:** shops, boutiques, cafes, restaurants, bakeries, food trucks, caterers,
anyone buying inventory or ingredients to sell.

To use it, copy everything under "Starter categories" below into
`../categories.md`, replacing whatever list is there.

## Starter categories

**Cost of what you sell (the money questions).**

- **Inventory / Goods for Resale (COGS)** -- product bought to sell on as-is.
- **Food & Beverage Cost (COGS)** -- ingredients, produce, meat, dairy, coffee,
  alcohol, anything that becomes what a customer buys.
- **Paper, Packaging & Serviceware (COGS)** -- cups, containers, bags, boxes,
  napkins, labels. Consumed per sale, so it belongs with cost of goods.
- **Kitchen & Store Supplies** -- cleaning supplies, gloves, small wares,
  utensils. Used up, but not part of a specific sale.

**Everything else.**

- **Equipment Repair & Maintenance** -- oven, cooler, espresso machine, POS
  hardware, refrigeration service. A repair shop is never Equipment Purchase.
- **Equipment Purchase (capital -- ask your accountant about depreciating)** --
  buying a cooler, oven, display case, or fit-out outright.
- **Rent & Facilities** -- store or kitchen rent, common charges, storage.
- **Utilities & Phone** -- electric, gas, water, internet, phone.
- **Wages-Adjacent Costs** -- uniforms, staff meals, hiring ads. (Payroll itself
  is not a receipt and does not belong in this ledger.)
- **Marketing & Advertising** -- ads, menus, signage, printing, sponsorships.
- **Software & Subscriptions** -- POS, delivery platforms, scheduling, AI tools.
- **Permits, Licenses & Inspections** -- health permit, liquor license, fire
  inspection, sidewalk permit.
- **Insurance** -- general liability, property, liquor liability.
- **Bank & Merchant Fees** -- card processing, delivery-platform commissions.
- **Professional Services** -- legal, accounting, consulting.
- **Vehicle & Fuel** -- delivery vehicle fuel, tolls, parking.
- **Other / Uncategorized**

## Judgment notes specific to this business

- The job field becomes the **location, event, or catering order** -- ask which
  store, truck, or event a purchase was for when more than one exists. If there
  is only one location, the installer can turn the job question off.
- A restaurant-supply or wholesale grocery run defaults to **Food & Beverage
  Cost (COGS)**; the same store's mop and gloves are **Kitchen & Store
  Supplies**. When a single receipt mixes both, ask how to split it or log it
  under the larger share and say so in the note.
- Any single item over $2,500 gets a capital-vs-expense note written into the
  row (see `../../SKILL.md`).
