# Category Variant -- Trades and Field Service

**For:** landscaping, hardscape, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing,
remodeling, general contracting, auto and truck shops, anyone who buys
materials for a customer's job.

**This is the default variant.** To use it, copy everything under "Starter
categories" below into `../categories.md`, replacing whatever list is there.

## Starter categories

**Job-related spend (the money questions).**

- **Job Materials (COGS)** -- anything consumed on a customer's job: lumber,
  pavers, mulch, pipe, wire, fixtures, concrete, stone, sod, fasteners,
  disposal of job debris. Cost of goods sold, usually the largest deduction a
  trade business has. If a job is open, hardware and supply-house spend lands
  here by default.
- **Subcontractors** -- another company or person paid to do part of a job.
- **Small Tools & Consumables** -- blades, bits, gloves, tarps, string line.
- **Equipment Repair & Maintenance** -- repairs and service on trucks,
  trailers, mowers, machines. A repair shop is never Vehicle & Fuel.
- **Equipment Purchase (capital -- ask your accountant about depreciating)** --
  buying a machine, truck, trailer, or attachment outright.
- **Permits & Filing Fees** -- town and county permits, inspections, licenses,
  dump tickets tied to a permit.
- **Crew Meals & Supplies** -- coffee and food for the crew, water, ice.

**Everything else.**

- **Vehicle & Fuel** -- fuel, tolls, parking, registration. Fuel only.
- **Office Supplies**
- **Software & Subscriptions** -- including estimating and scheduling tools.
- **Marketing & Advertising** -- ads, truck lettering, yard signs, printing.
- **Professional Services** -- legal, accounting, consulting.
- **Utilities & Phone** -- shop or yard.
- **Rent & Facilities** -- shop, yard, storage.
- **Insurance** -- general liability, vehicle, workers comp.
- **Education & Training** -- license renewals, courses, certifications.
- **Bank & Merchant Fees**
- **Client Meals**
- **Other / Uncategorized**

## Judgment notes specific to this trade

- A supply house, lumberyard, or big-box hardware store on a day with an open
  job defaults to **Job Materials (COGS)**, not Office Supplies or Equipment.
- A repair shop, tire shop, or parts counter is **Equipment Repair &
  Maintenance**, never Vehicle & Fuel, even though a truck is involved.
- Any single item over $2,500 gets a capital-vs-expense note written into the
  row (see `../../SKILL.md`).
- On a New York job, a capital-improvement contract may be exempt from sales tax
  under form ST-124 -- if a materials receipt shows sales tax on what looks like
  a capital improvement, mention it once so the owner can raise it with their
  accountant. Do not state whether it applies; that is the accountant's call.
